# System Usability Scale — Participant P[ ]

The SUS (Brooke, 1996) is the standard usability questionnaire. Using it rather
than a homemade rating scale means your numbers can be compared against a
published benchmark, which is worth more in the Analysis section than an
invented five-star score.

Hand it over **after** the interview, and leave the room or stay quiet while it
is completed. Ask for a gut reaction to each item rather than deliberation.

---

## Questionnaire

For each statement, circle one:
**1 = Strongly disagree · 2 = Disagree · 3 = Neutral · 4 = Agree · 5 = Strongly agree**

| # | Statement | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1 | I think I would like to use this app frequently. | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| 2 | I found this app unnecessarily complex. | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| 3 | I thought this app was easy to use. | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| 4 | I think I would need help from a technical person to use this app. | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| 5 | I found the various functions in this app were well integrated. | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| 6 | I thought there was too much inconsistency in this app. | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| 7 | I would imagine most people would learn to use this app very quickly. | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| 8 | I found this app very cumbersome to use. | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| 9 | I felt very confident using this app. | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ |
| 10 | I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this app. | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ | ○ |

Odd-numbered items are worded positively and even-numbered negatively on
purpose — it stops people ticking straight down one column without reading.

---

## Scoring

1. **Odd items (1,3,5,7,9):** score = response − 1
2. **Even items (2,4,6,8,10):** score = 5 − response
3. Add all ten scores (range 0–40)
4. **Multiply by 2.5** → SUS score out of 100

It is not a percentage. A SUS of 70 does not mean 70% of anything.

Also worth reporting: the **range**. A mean of 75 from scores of 74 and 76 tells

distinction is the kind of thing the Analysis band rewards.
