# Post-Session Interview — Participant P[ ]

Ask **after** the tasks, before the survey. Keep it conversational, roughly five
minutes. These are prompts, not a script — follow anything interesting.

Two rules that decide whether this is worth anything:

- **Never defend the app.** The moment you explain why something works the way it does, the participant stops criticising it.
- **Ask "why" one more time than feels comfortable.** The first answer is usually politeness; the second is the finding.

---

**Moderator note.** Question 16 is the most honest signal in the set — a polite
participant will praise an app they would never actually use, and the reasoning
behind a "no" is usually the most valuable thing in the whole session.
