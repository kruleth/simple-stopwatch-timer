## What this is

You are being asked to help test a mobile app built as part of a university
coursework project. The session takes about **20–30 minutes**.

You will be asked to carry out a few everyday tasks with the app — timing
something, setting a countdown, looking back at a past result — and to say what
you are thinking as you go. Afterwards there are a few short questions and a
ten-item questionnaire.

## The app is being tested, not you

There are no right or wrong answers and nothing you can get wrong. If something
is confusing or awkward, that is a problem with the app's design and is exactly
what this session is for. Saying so is the most useful thing you can do.

## What is recorded

- Written notes on what you do and say
- [Screen recording / audio recording] — **only if you agree below**
- Your answers to the questions and questionnaire

Some things you say may be quoted in the coursework report.

## How your information is handled

- You are identified only as **"P[number]"**. Your name is never written down alongside your responses.
- Quotes are attributed to the pseudonym only.
- Basic background (age band, occupation, how confident you are with technology) is recorded, because variety among participants matters to the study. None of it identifies you.
- Recordings and notes are kept on the researcher's own device, used solely for this coursework, and deleted after the module is marked.
- Nothing is published anywhere beyond the coursework submission.

## Your choices

- Taking part is entirely voluntary.
- You can skip any task or question without giving a reason.
- You can stop at any point, and you can ask for your data to be destroyed afterwards — no explanation needed.
